How it's actually validated

Nothing gets promoted to live on a good-looking backtest alone.

Walk-forward, not just backtest

Every candidate config is evaluated out-of-sample across rolling folds before it's even considered for promotion.

Hard statistical gates

Minimum trade counts, minimum profit factor, and win-rate floors on both bull and bear windows — not just "profit > 0".

Self-evolving hypothesis engine

The brain generates, tests, and prunes its own experiments continuously — hundreds of them — and the honest failures get written down, not hidden.

Non-custodial by design

The planned service model never touches your funds — a thin executor runs against your own exchange API keys, scoped to trading only.

Where things honestly stand

Cortexa is dry-run only right now — no real capital is being traded. The research process has been unusually rigorous for a solo project: every failed hypothesis is written down, not swept under the rug, and nothing gets promoted without clearing real out-of-sample bars.

That rigor is the point. A system that tells you the truth about what isn't working yet is worth more than one that hides it — and it's how we'll know the moment something genuinely is.
